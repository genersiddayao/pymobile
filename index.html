It takes less than a minute to run your first program.

  1. Open the appTap Launch the app. It works in Chrome, Brave, Safari or Edge.

  3. Tap RunOutput appears instantly. Use Add to Home screen to keep it like an app.

Many of my students learn programming without owning a laptop, and the popular Python apps for phones lock useful features behind a paid upgrade. I built PyMobile IDE so that no student is held back by that.

It's free for every CSU campus, college and program that teaches Python, and for anyone else who wants to learn.
